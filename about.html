ABOUT US

Who We Are

Welcome to Sujnana National Public School

Sujnana National Public School, managed by the prestigious Shri Yalaguresh Educational Trust (R), is dedicated to offering a highly intellectual and value-based education system. Our campus offers students a vibrant learning ecosystem focused on personal growth, creativity, and modern skills development.

We believe that education must go beyond textbook knowledge. We nurture our students' emotional quotient, artistic expression, and physical capability, preparing them to be compassionate global citizens who can contribute constructively to our society.

Our Vision

To establish an inspiring space for intellectual discovery where students are empowered with high moral values, critical analysis capabilities, and strong leadership attributes to shape a better global society.

Our Mission

To provide a structured and advanced academic program matching current technological shifts while encouraging emotional growth, physical fitness, self-confidence, and ethical standards through dedicated mentoring.

Milestones

Our Proud History

2001

The Foundation Stones

Shri Yalaguresh Educational Trust (R) laid the foundation for the school campus with a small block of primary classrooms.

2008

High School Accreditation

The school expanded its academic classes up to Grade 10, receiving high school authorization from government boards.

2015

Smart Campus Upgrade

Smartboards were introduced across all senior classrooms along with modern physics, chemistry, and biology laboratory upgrades.

2024

Silver Jubilee Celebrations

The school successfully celebrated its milestones of academic excellence and holistic child development support.
