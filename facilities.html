Facilities

Home Facilities

We provide state-of-the-art facilities and resources to ensure holistic development and academic excellence for every student.

Smart Classrooms

Digital learning environment with interactive whiteboards, overhead projectors, and modern audio systems to encourage student participation.

Science Laboratories

Well-equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology labs with precise practical apparatus, safety systems, and guided demo support.

Computer Lab

High-speed computers with up-to-date program modules, visual aids, coding platforms, and secure firewall networking.

Library

A vast collection of educational syllabus books, fiction, scientific journals, reference materials, and quiet study areas.

Sports Facilities

Outdoor playground rings for cricket, volleyball, track fields, alongside indoor tables for chess and carrom matches.

Transport Facility

Safe and reliable transport vehicles covering all major neighborhoods, accompanied by CCTV trackers and experienced staff.

Auditorium

Spacious soundproof indoor auditorium hosting parents meetings, co-curricular arts, and inter-school seminars.

Cafeteria

Clean, hygienic canteen serving nutritious meals, hot drinks, snacks, and mineral water under absolute supervision.

World-class facilities for holistic development

Our campus is built to encourage creative inquiry, critical thinking, physical fitness, and technological fluency in every young learner.
