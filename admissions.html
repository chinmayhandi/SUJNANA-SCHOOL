How It Works

Admission Process Flow

01

Enquiry Submission

Fill out our online Admission Enquiry Form or visit the campus helpdesk directly.

02

Document Verification

Submit required certificates and school papers for administration verification.

03

Interaction / Assessment

A brief friendly conversation with parent and basic assessment session for the student.

04

Admission Confirmation

The school releases an admission offer letter based on class vacancies and assessment results.

05

Fee Payment

Submit terms fees, complete documentation steps, and secure the student's seat.

Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate (issued by Municipal Corporation)
  • Student Aadhaar Card copy
  • 4 Passport Size Photos of student
  • Previous School Report Card (Grade 1 onwards)
  • Original Transfer Certificate (TC) if applicable
