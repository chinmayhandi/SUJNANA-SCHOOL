Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 26 June 2026

At Sujnana National Public School, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, parent name, student name, phone number, email address, class of interest and message details when you fill enquiry or contact forms.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, provide admission-related information, communicate school updates and improve website services.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information may be shared only when required by law or with trusted service providers.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve browsing experience and website performance. Users can disable cookies in browser settings.

External Links

Our website may contain external links. We are not responsible for privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Children’s Privacy

Parents or guardians should submit student information when required. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without proper consent.

Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with the updated effective date.

Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, please contact the school through the Contact page.

Questions about Privacy?

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
