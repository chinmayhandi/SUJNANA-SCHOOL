Easy Access Guide

Sujnana National Public School campus is conveniently accessible from neighboring districts.

  • By Bus: 2.5 km away from Muddebihal main bus terminus. Direct sharing auto-rickshaws run frequently to the campus entrance gates.
  • By Road: Situated directly on Muddebihal-Talikoti road connection line, providing smooth highway access to private vehicles.
  • By Train: Almatti Railway Station is the nearest rail connection (approx 22 km away) linking passenger trains.
